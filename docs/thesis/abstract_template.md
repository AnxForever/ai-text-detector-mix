## 中文摘要

**题目**：基于深度学习的中文AI生成文本检测方法研究

**摘要**：

随着ChatGPT、GPT-4等大语言模型的快速发展，AI生成的中文文本在质量上已接近人类水平，这给学术诚信、内容审核和知识产权保护带来了严峻挑战。本文针对中文AI文本检测问题，开展了系统的研究工作。

首先，构建了覆盖教育学术、职场办公、知识百科、社区互动、商业营销、新闻资讯6个应用场景的多场景中文AI文本检测数据集，包含约6万条样本，涵盖ChatGPT、GPT-4、DeepSeek、GLM、Qwen等10余个主流AI模型生成的文本。

其次，提出了基于BERT的中文AI文本检测方法。该方法利用预训练语言模型的语义表示能力，通过分类头实现AI文本与人类文本的二分类。针对"人类开头+AI续写"的混合文本，提出[SEP]边界标记机制，有效提升混合文本的检测能力。

实验结果表明，本文方法在测试集上取得了98.71%的准确率和98.77%的F1分数。长度免疫性测试中，模型在所有长度区间均达到100%准确率，性能方差为0，证明学习的是真实语义特征而非长度偏差。混合文本检测中，C2续写检测率达93.84%，相比无边界标记提升14个百分点。

本文的研究成果为中文AI文本检测提供了有效的解决方案，具有重要的理论价值和实际应用意义。

**关键词**：AI生成文本检测；大语言模型；BERT；中文自然语言处理；深度学习

## English Abstract

**Title**: Research on Chinese AI-Generated Text Detection Methods Based on Deep Learning

**Abstract**:

With the rapid development of large language models such as ChatGPT and GPT-4, AI-generated Chinese text has approached human-level quality, posing serious challenges to academic integrity, content moderation, and intellectual property protection. This thesis conducts systematic research on Chinese AI text detection.

First, we construct a multi-scenario Chinese AI text detection dataset covering six application scenarios: education, workplace, knowledge, community, commerce, and news. The dataset contains approximately 60,000 samples generated by more than 10 mainstream AI models including ChatGPT, GPT-4, DeepSeek, GLM, and Qwen.

Second, we propose a BERT-based Chinese AI text detection method. This method leverages the semantic representation capability of pre-trained language models to achieve binary classification between AI-generated and human-written text. For mixed text with "human beginning + AI continuation", we propose a [SEP] boundary marking mechanism to effectively improve detection capability.

Experimental results show that our method achieves 98.71% accuracy and 98.77% F1 score on the test set. In length immunity tests, the model achieves 100% accuracy across all length intervals with zero performance variance, demonstrating that it learns genuine semantic features rather than length bias. For mixed text detection, the C2 continuation detection rate reaches 93.84%, an improvement of 14 percentage points compared to the baseline without boundary markers.

The research results of this thesis provide an effective solution for Chinese AI text detection, with significant theoretical value and practical application significance.

**Keywords**: AI-generated text detection; Large language models; BERT; Chinese natural language processing; Deep learning
