# V11d2 Gemini Patch Decision Report (2026-02-13)

## Patch Summary

V11d2 is the second iteration of the Gemini-search patch, adjusting V11d's data strategy to attempt reducing FP regressions.

- Training mode: **fast** (1 epoch from V11c, LR=3e-6)
- Data: `train_v11d2_candidate.csv` (63,427 rows, +240 vs V11c)
- Supplement: 80 AI (56 Gemini-related + 24 Gemini-search) + 160 Human (LCSTS-news:70, external_m4_qazh:70, 模板-通知:20)
- Training time: ~29 min on RTX 5060
- Temperature: T=0.9041 (calibrated on independent_data, ECE 0.0183 -> 0.0096)

## Regression Gate

- Decision: **rollback_to_baseline** (FAIL)
- three_set_avg degradation: 0.60% > 0.50% threshold
- This is the first V11d-series candidate to trigger the hard rollback gate

## Root Cause Analysis

The V11d2 strategy of lowering LR (3e-6) and diversifying human sources **backfired**:

1. **Lower LR didn't reduce FP**: The learning rate reduction from 5e-6 to 3e-6 was insufficient to prevent decision boundary shift. FP actually increased from 17 (V11d) to 25 (V11d2).

2. **Human source diversity didn't help**: Adding external_m4_qazh and 模板-通知 samples to the supplement was intended to anchor the human distribution, but it appears to have introduced distribution shift in the opposite direction.

3. **New Toutiao_News regression**: V11d2 created a new regression in Toutiao_News (98.64% vs 100%) that didn't exist in either V11c or V11d. This suggests the supplement data is disrupting patterns beyond the Gemini/formal domains.

4. **Compounding effect**: V11d2 is worse than V11d on every metric except merged_v2_val_clean (tied). The conservative LR approach did not translate to conservative model behavior.

## Decision

### **Keep V11c (no change)**

- Continue with `bert_v11c_boundary_fix` with T=0.8165
- Rationale: V11d2 fails 5 of 6 acceptance criteria, fails the regression gate hard threshold, and introduces new regressions (Toutiao_News) that V11d didn't have
- The Gemini-search 7/8 regression in V11c is an acceptable known limitation
- V11c remains the best overall model with three-set average 98.56%

### Assessment: fast-mode patching is not viable for Gemini-search

Both V11d (LR=5e-6) and V11d2 (LR=3e-6) demonstrate that single-epoch fast-mode fine-tuning from V11c cannot fix Gemini-search without degrading formal_collected and overall precision. The Gemini-search samples create a strong enough gradient signal to shift the decision boundary even at very low learning rates.

**Future options (if Gemini-search 8/8 is required):**
- Full retrain from V7 baseline with Gemini data in the original training mix (not a patch)
- Ensemble approach: V11c for general classification + specialized Gemini detector
- Accept V11c's 7/8 as the production trade-off
