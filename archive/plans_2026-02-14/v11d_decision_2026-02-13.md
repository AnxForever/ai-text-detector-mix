# V11d Gemini Patch Decision Report (2026-02-13)

## Patch Summary

V11d is a targeted data supplement on top of V11c to recover the `gemini-3-pro-preview-search` regression (7/8 in V11c).

- Training mode: **fast** (1 epoch from V11c, LR=5e-6)
- Data: `train_v11d_candidate.csv` (63,427 rows, +240 vs V11c)
- Supplement: 120 AI (30 Gemini-search + 90 Gemini-family) + 120 Human (LCSTS-news)
- Training time: ~29 min on RTX 5060
- Temperature: T=0.8913 (calibrated on independent_data, ECE 0.0141 -> 0.0030)

## Trade-off Analysis

**V11d gains**:
- Gemini-search: 7/8 -> 8/8 (primary objective achieved)
- external_m4_qazh: 95.92% -> 97.96%
- Recall improved: 98.67% -> 99.33% (fewer AI texts missed)
- ECE slightly better on calibration set

**V11d costs**:
- Three-set average: 98.56% -> 98.29% (-0.27%)
- formal_collected: 96.50% -> 93.00% (-3.50%, +7 FP)
- Precision: 93.08% -> 89.76% (-3.32%)
- Total errors: 13 -> 18 (+5)

## Decision Options

### Option A: Promote V11d

- Deploy `bert_v11d_gemini_patch` with T=0.8913
- Rationale: Primary objective (Gemini-search 8/8) achieved; regression gate passed; three-set avg still strong at 98.29%
- Risk: formal_collected FP increase (+7) may affect user experience on formal/academic human texts

### Option B: Keep V11c (no change)

- Continue with `bert_v11c_boundary_fix` with T=0.8165
- Rationale: Three-set average drop (0.27%) exceeds the strict 0.20% handoff threshold; FP increase is concerning
- Risk: Gemini-search remains at 7/8 (known regression)

### Option C: Retrain V11d full mode

- Run `py -3 scripts/training/train_v11d.py --mode full` (5 epochs from V7 baseline)
- Rationale: Full retrain may find a better optimum than single-epoch fine-tune
- Risk: Longer training time; no guarantee of improvement
